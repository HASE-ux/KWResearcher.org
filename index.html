How this keyword tool works

Live suggestions

When your browser can reach Google autocomplete, real suggested searches are pulled for your seed, with deep search expanding the best results again.

Related terms

Related words are looked up to add different angles on your topic, not just the same phrase with a new ending.

Topic-aware ideas

Your seed is matched to a topic (shopping, health, tech, finance, learning, food and travel) so ideas fit the niche. Near-duplicates are removed.

Clusters and intent

Ideas are grouped by topic and labelled informational, commercial or transactional. The mixed order spreads similar phrases apart.

Keyword research guide

Find low competition keywords

Prefer long-tail phrases of four or more words and question keywords. Search the phrase on Google and check whether the top results are strong pages or weak, outdated ones.


Match search intent

Informational keywords suit blog posts, commercial ones suit comparison pages and transactional ones suit product or service pages. No tool can guarantee rankings.


Build clusters

Group related keywords that share a topic and intent, then write one strong page per cluster instead of many thin pages.

Frequently asked questions

Is KeywordPro AI really free?

Yes. There is no signup, no limit on searches and no paywall. Everything runs in your browser.

Does it show search volume and keyword difficulty?

No. Real volume and difficulty need paid data sources, so this tool does not invent them. Each keyword links to Google Trends so you can check demand yourself.

Where do the keyword ideas come from?

Ideas come from Google autocomplete and related-word lookups when your browser can reach them, plus topic-aware pattern ideas. Each row shows its source.

Can I export the keywords?

Yes. Download CSV or JSON, copy the list, or save favourites and compare them in Google Trends.
